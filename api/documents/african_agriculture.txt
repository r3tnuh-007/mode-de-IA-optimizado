Cassava (Manihot esculenta) is a fundamental crop for food security in Africa. It is drought-resistant and can be grown in poor soils.

Maize is the primary food crop in Tanzania, Kenya, and Uganda. Maize production in East Africa is affected by pests such as the fall armyworm.

Precision agriculture uses sensors, GPS, and data analysis to optimize the use of water, fertilizers, and pesticides, increasing productivity and reducing costs.

No-till farming is a soil conservation technique that reduces erosion, increases water retention, and improves soil fertility over the long term.

Crop rotation helps break the cycle of diseases and pests, maintaining soil health and increasing productivity.
